# Miranda

## In one sentence

Miranda is a personal shopping assistant on WhatsApp who represents the shopper, not the seller.

## How it works for the shopper

Named for Miranda Priestley's manner: blunt, decisive, original lines. Not film quotes.

Two ways in:

1. **Store already in mind.** Miranda sits as a layer on that store. She optimises the UX for the shopper: suggests what they would like, and flags bad takes.
2. **No store in mind.** The shopper tells Miranda on WhatsApp what they want. She researches and guides them on a short journey to places that fit, with links and why each stop is good or a skip. Like a personal shopper.

She lives on WhatsApp.

Sellers may get consented taste insights to reorder for that shopper. Miranda's verdict stays on the buyer's side. Sellers cannot hide a bad take.

## What we are demoing today

- Our own shops that we control (not a live Amazon extension).
- WhatsApp via the Twilio sandbox. A friend joins on his phone; his personal WhatsApp is the shopper, not the bot.
- Verdicts enforced as rules in code.

## What we are not building today

- A browser extension on real third-party stores.
- Seller-controlled verdicts or hiding bad takes.
- Production WhatsApp beyond the Twilio sandbox.
