# Grok Bot Commerce London Hackathon

Source: https://luma.com/cursor-td9f?tk=RsW4cG

- **Date:** Saturday, 26 September 2026 (one day)
- **Venue:** Fleek HQ, 20-22 Commercial St, London E1 6LP
- **Host org:** SpaceXAI for London
- **Theme:** Build agentic commerce — "when bots become active leads in commerce"
- **Stack called out:** Cursor, Grok Bots, Origin, Supabase (also mentions Shopify + "modern commerce APIs")
- **Teams:** solo or up to 3

## Suggested idea areas

- Shopping Bots
- Autonomous Commerce
- Smart Merchants and Marketplaces
- Smart Payments & Checkout
- Conversational Commerce
- "The Weird Stuff" — things only possible when AI bots + commerce infra are designed together from day one

Pitch lines from the page: *build a bot that shops for you, a merchant that runs itself, a new kind of marketplace, or something we haven't seen before.*

## Judging criteria

1. Technical execution
2. Product thinking
3. AI leverage & autonomy
4. Commerce innovation
5. Real-world usefulness
6. UX
7. Demo quality

Prizes: 1st / 2nd / 3rd plus sponsor prizes and special awards. Every team submits a working demo at code freeze; Top 5 present live.

## Questions the organisers want answered

- What does a storefront look like when the customer is an AI?
- How does a merchant sell to a bot rather than a person?
- Can a bot negotiate a purchase?
- Can bots discover and transact with other bots?
- How should humans authorize autonomous purchases?
- What happens to search when bots decide what products to show?
- What does customer loyalty mean when your bot chooses where you buy?
- How do we make autonomous commerce trustworthy and observable?
- What entirely new businesses become possible?
