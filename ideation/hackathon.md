# Grok Bot Commerce London Hackathon

Sources:
- **Official page (source of truth):** https://gb-ecommerce-hackathon-09-2026.teamdeel.workers.dev/hackathon
- Luma listing (earlier, partly out of date): https://luma.com/cursor-td9f?tk=RsW4cG

Where the two disagree, the official page wins. The Luma-only details are kept at the bottom for context.

- **Date:** Saturday, 26 September 2026 (one day)
- **Venue:** Fleek HQ, 20-22 Commercial St, London E1 6LP
- **Tagline:** "Build the next generation of commerce experiences."
- **Teams:** solo or up to 3 (from Luma)
- **Primary stack tools (official page):** Shopify, Commerce Layer, Recharge, Sanity, PostHog, plus Cursor, Grok Bot, Supabase, Tavily and Vercel as tech partners

## Tracks (official)

Pick one, or blend buyer, seller and merchant tooling.

| Track | Brief |
| --- | --- |
| **Storefront Experience** | Better ways to discover, explore and buy products |
| **Buyer Experience** | Help customers understand products and make better buying decisions |
| **Merchant Tooling** | Tools that help merchants manage, optimise and grow their stores |
| **New Ways to Buy** | Subscriptions, bundles, marketplaces, reselling and programmatic purchasing |
| **Agentic Commerce** | Experiences where AI agents can discover, compare and transact |

## Judging criteria (official)

1. **Problem:** is the commerce problem clear and worth solving?
2. **Experience:** buyer, seller or merchant impact
3. **Execution:** does the prototype work well?
4. **AI / Grok Bot:** meaningful use of agents
5. **Commerce depth:** real understanding of the stack
6. **Originality:** fresh or distinctive idea
7. **Impact:** plausible business or customer value

## Demo format

- Every team submits a **live demo** at code freeze.
- The Top 5 highest-scoring teams present live (3 minutes each).
- Judges award 1st, 2nd and 3rd from the finalists.
- The official page has "Submit" and "Judge Portal" buttons; there's no public submit URL, so use the button on the page on the day.

## Luma-only details (older, kept for context)

The Luma listing framed the theme as "when bots become active leads in commerce", said the host org was SpaceXAI for London, and listed different tracks (Marketplaces, Shopping, Merchants, Infrastructure, Bonus Sponsor Challenges) and a different schedule (hacking 09:35, code freeze 16:45). **Use the official tracks and times above.**

Suggested idea areas from Luma: Shopping Bots, Autonomous Commerce, Smart Merchants and Marketplaces, Smart Payments & Checkout, Conversational Commerce, "The Weird Stuff". Pitch line: *build a bot that shops for you, a merchant that runs itself, a new kind of marketplace, or something we haven't seen before.*

Questions the organisers want answered (Luma), still a useful framing:

- What does a storefront look like when the customer is an AI?
- How does a merchant sell to a bot rather than a person?
- Can a bot negotiate a purchase?
- Can bots discover and transact with other bots?
- How should humans authorize autonomous purchases?
- What happens to search when bots decide what products to show?
- What does customer loyalty mean when your bot chooses where you buy?
- How do we make autonomous commerce trustworthy and observable?
- What entirely new businesses become possible?
